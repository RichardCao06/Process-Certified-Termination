## Explicit exclusions

No claim currently covers:

- other DeepSeek Harness commits as equivalent;
- other worker models or providers;
- Claude Code, Codex, or other harnesses;
- high-stakes production deployment;
- private chain-of-thought monitoring;
- tasks without observable evidence;
- irreversible actions without human approval.

## Expansion rule

The envelope expands only after preregistered external-validity tests. Directional consistency does not prove universal generality; heterogeneity, cost, failure modes, and task-specific limits must be reported.
